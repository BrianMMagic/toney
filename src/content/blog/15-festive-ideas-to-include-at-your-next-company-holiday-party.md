Planning a company holiday party requires thoughtful consideration. Research indicates that "only 36 percent of professionals feel that holiday parties are fun." Creating a unique atmosphere can help engage the remaining majority who don't typically enjoy these events.

## 1. Hire Entertainment

Professional entertainers eliminate awkward silences by providing engaging performances. Options include:

- **Live Band:** Select musicians capable of playing diverse songs for dancing and enjoyment
- **Magician:** Offers unique entertainment at a more affordable price point than bands
- **Standup Comedian:** Provides laughter, though content should be appropriate for all attendees
- **Mixologist:** Creates specialty craft cocktails with holiday themes

## 2. Pick a Theme

A cohesive theme increases participation and excitement. Popular options include:

- **Winter Wonderland:** Feature artificial snow, twinkling lights, and white decorations
- **Holiday Movie Theme:** Base the party around classics like *The Grinch* or *A Charlie Brown Christmas*
- **Decade Party:** Combine holiday attire from a specific era (1950s, 1980s, etc.)
- **Ugly Sweater Party:** A timeless choice that remains entertaining

Involve employees by voting on the theme and coordinating decorations, favors, refreshments, and dress codes accordingly.

## 3. Have Games and Prizes

Interactive games maintain energy throughout the event. Holiday-themed options include:

- Gingerbread house building contests
- Holiday-themed charades
- Christmas movie trivia
- Christmas song karaoke
- Ornament or cookie decorating
- Scavenger hunts

Offer desirable prizes to encourage participation:

- Movie tickets
- Gift cards (Target, Amazon)
- Coffee mugs with treats
- Tech gadgets
- Fancy chocolates
- Restaurant dinner certificates

## 4. Switch Up the Gift Exchange

Traditional Secret Santa can feel repetitive. Consider alternatives:

- **Dessert Swap:** Participants bring holiday treats and draw names
- **Themed Gift Exchange:** Establish specific categories (books, edible items, etc.)
- **Guessing Game:** Attendees win mystery packages by correctly identifying contents
- **Charitable Giving:** Direct gift money toward organizations instead

## 5. Schedule a Group Outing

Plan activities during work hours to boost morale and strengthen team connections. Options include:

- Ice skating
- Holiday light tours via limousine
- Comedy shows or sports events
- Laser tag
- Escape rooms

Book venues well in advance to accommodate group sizes.
