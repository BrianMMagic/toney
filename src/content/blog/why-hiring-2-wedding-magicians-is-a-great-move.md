If you're planning a wedding and looking for unique and memorable entertainment, hiring two magicians could be the perfect solution. Not only will a wedding magician add a touch of wonder and excitement to your special day, but having two performers will allow for even more variety and spectacle.

## Types of Magic for Weddings

First and foremost, it's important to consider what kind of magic you want at your wedding. Close-up magic, also known as sleight of hand, is a type of magic that occurs within arm's reach of the audience and often involves cards, coins, and other small objects. This type of magic is great for cocktail hour or during dinner, as it allows the magician to interact with guests and perform for small groups.

On the other hand, stage magic is a larger-scale type of performance that involves grand illusions and is typically done in front of a larger audience. This might be a good option for your reception, as it can provide a wow factor and keep guests entertained during breaks in the festivities.

## Finding the Right Magicians

Once you've decided on the type of magic you want at your wedding, it's time to start looking for magicians. There are many talented wedding magicians out there, but it's important to do your research and choose performers who are professional, experienced, and have good reviews.

When considering hiring two magicians, think about how their styles and specialties complement each other. For example, one magician might be skilled in close-up magic while the other excels at stage illusions. Alternatively, you could hire one magician to perform close-up magic during cocktail hour and the other to do stage magic during the reception.

## Benefits of Two Magicians

Having two magicians at your wedding also allows for more flexibility in terms of scheduling. If you have a long reception, having two magicians means that one can take a break while the other performs, ensuring that your guests are entertained throughout the entire event.

In addition to hiring magicians, there are other ways to incorporate magic into your wedding entertainment. For example, you could have a magician emcee the event, adding just a touch of magic to the announcements and transitions throughout the evening. You could also have a magician hang out near your photo booth to wow them while they're waiting for their photo.

## Conclusion

No matter how you choose to incorporate magic into your wedding entertainment, hiring two magicians will add a unique and memorable touch to your special day. Not only will it provide entertainment for your guests, but it will also create a sense of whimsy and wonder that will make your wedding truly unforgettable.
