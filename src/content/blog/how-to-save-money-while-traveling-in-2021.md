My wife and I have learned valuable lessons about travel spending over our many trips together. Rather than wasting money on unnecessary purchases, we prioritize experiences. These money-saving strategies come from our own personal travel mistakes — learn from us!

## Don't Pay Full Price for Airline Tickets

Flight costs often intimidate potential travelers. I recommend using Scott's Cheap Flights, a service that identifies discounted airfare deals. Examples include Spain for $350, Ireland for $425, Japan for $475, Italy for $480, and London for $450 round-trip. Premium membership costs $40 annually and provides early access to deals plus airport filtering options.

## Ditch the Giant Bags

Checking luggage on connecting flights adds substantial fees. The solution: travel with only a carry-on backpack. This approach prevents lost bags, avoids rummaging concerns, and discourages purchasing unnecessary souvenirs.

## Eat Out Once a Day

Rather than dining out for every meal, eat breakfast at your accommodation and pack picnic lunches. Reserve restaurant visits for one meal daily. An additional tip: visit upscale restaurants during lunch hours when prices are considerably lower.

## Not Traveling Alone? Use Airbnb

Hotels charge premium rates internationally. Airbnb offers budget-friendly alternatives from shared rooms to private apartments. User reviews ensure transparency about accommodations before booking.

## Travel Off-Season

Visiting destinations during their slower periods reduces crowds and costs. Peak seasons like summer and Christmas inflate prices across all travel categories, making off-season travel economically advantageous.

## Use Public Transportation

Taxis prove expensive and potentially unreliable. Using local transit systems like subways saves substantial money. Planning routes beforehand eliminates stress and prevents overspending on transportation.

## Book Attraction Tickets Online Ahead of Time

Online booking typically saves at least 20% compared to on-site purchases and eliminates ticket line wait times. Timed entry requirements necessitate schedule planning.

## Sightseeing City Pass

Many major cities offer bundled attraction passes. The strategy involves comparing individual admission costs against pass prices. When beneficial, these passes save approximately $40 or more.

## Take Overnight Trains to Your Next Destination

Sleeper trains combine accommodation and transportation into one expense. These tickets typically cost comparable to single hotel nights while eliminating wasted daylight hours.

## Skip the International Fees

Credit card companies often charge international transaction fees. Selecting a no-fee card and alerting your bank to travel plans prevents billing surprises.

## Conclusion

Apply these strategies to maximize your travel budget, ensuring money stretches further for meaningful experiences rather than unnecessary expenses. Happy travels!
