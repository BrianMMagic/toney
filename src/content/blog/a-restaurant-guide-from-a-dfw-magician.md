Great food can be found anywhere from a food truck, to fine dining, and everywhere in between. As a Dallas-Fort Worth magician who's always out and about for events, I've developed strong opinions about the local dining scene. Here are some of my favorite spots — I'm sharing these to support small businesses during challenging economic times.

## Namoo Korean Bowl

Located on Abram Street in Arlington, this Korean restaurant offers an inviting atmosphere with K-pop music videos playing throughout. I recommend the spring rolls and the Porki-Poki bowl, both affordably priced around $9 per entree. Complimentary miso soup is available for all diners.

**Address:** 300 E Abram, Unit 170, Arlington

## Kura Revolving Sushi

After experiencing revolving sushi during my Japan honeymoon, I discovered this Plano location by chance. The unique concept features items on a conveyor belt passing each table; you select plates you want. iPads at tables allow ordering specialty hot items. They use "fresh, high quality ingredients in their recipes that competitors simply cannot match."

Multiple locations operate across the DFW area and beyond.

## Pie Tap Pizza Workshop + Bar

This hipster-style pizzeria in the Dallas Design District is my favorite pizza destination. My signature order is the Prosciutto pizza, featuring medjool dates, pistachio, arugula, balsamic glaze, and dual cheeses — an unconventional combination I initially tried reluctantly but now love. The garlic knots appetizers are also highly recommended.

## Piranha Killer Ramen

This newer Arlington Highlands location features beautiful Japanese murals and traditional Japanese-style seating. I frequent the happy hour weekly and consistently order Shoyu Ramen with added bok choy. The menu includes crispy rice with tuna and gyoza dumplings.

**Address:** 309 Curtis Mathes Way, Arlington

## Twisted Root Burger Co.

An industrial "garage"-themed burger establishment with locations throughout DFW, Twisted Root offers premium ground beef burgers. I recommend the "Kevin Bacon" burger and their unique pickle bar featuring five to six flavors. Custom sodas and homemade shakes round out the menu.

## Marugame Udon

Inspired by my 2017 Japan visit, I loved discovering authentic udon noodles at this Texas location. The beef udon features outstanding broth topped with tempura crumbs and green onions. Unfortunately, this restaurant permanently closed its doors, leaving the California and Japan locations as alternatives.

---

I hope you get a chance to explore some of these local spots and support small businesses creating memorable dining experiences. And now, back to my primary profession — mentalism and magic!
