## The Genesis of a Trick

The magic trick's creation began in July 2021 with brainstorming sessions. By July 2022, a professional craftsperson was enlisted to develop quality props, replacing initial homemade versions. Training intensified in August, with a family member joining by October. Professional props arrived in February 2023, followed by heightened rehearsal efforts to prepare the submission video.

## The Call to the Stage

The production process accelerated in early March 2023. After submitting the act on March 7th, producers acknowledged receipt within days. On April 6th, the show's team expressed genuine interest and requested a detailed script along with filming schedule information. By May 7th, producers called to discuss the trick's mechanics and backstory. The pivotal moment arrived on May 15th when "official confirmation of our appearance on the show" was received.

June involved script revisions. A July 6th production meeting brought "performance adjustments and invaluable advice." Details were finalized regarding props and staging. The narrative focus shifted on July 13th from corporate entertainment to "a more intriguing angle centered on my spooky collection."

## Pre-Vegas Preparations

Final preparations emphasized precise table and chair heights. Late July guidance covered appropriate attire and wardrobe selections. The team requested "five talking points for our post-performance interaction with Brooke Burke."

## Viva Las Vegas

The Vegas trip began August 7th following three strolling performances that left my voice strained. The August 8th interview filming marked a significant milestone in the televised experience.

## Showtime!

The live performance occurred August 9th. Despite equipment challenges and dry-mouth anxiety, the act succeeded. The crew praised it as "one of the funniest of the season," and Teller personally congratulated me backstage.

## The Broadcast

The episode aired November 3rd. I had a prior booking that evening but later watched with family during a celebration.

This experience represents the beginning of a larger journey creating television-focused magic performances. You can watch the full performance on YouTube — search for "Anthony Dempsey Fool Us" to find it!
