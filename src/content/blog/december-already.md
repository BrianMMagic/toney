Wow, I can honestly say that this past 8 weeks has been a blur. Between doing weddings on the weekends, working my usual corporate event magician dates during the week, and writing an all-new stage magic show when I have a little spare time- I'm pretty beat! My meals consisted of a lot of frozen prepped meals, protein bars and drive thru food, but I made it! I loved the momentum and fast pace of it all. I'd do it again in a heartbeat!

Oh, did I mention that I even squeezed in a week-long trip to Frankenmuth, Michigan? This place is so amazing, especially during the Christmas season. If you're looking to truly get into the Christmas spirit, put Frankenmuth on your bucket list! They even have an excellent hotel suitable for corporate events.

Now that the holiday season is fast approaching (what am I saying- the holidays are HERE!), I'm already preparing as much as I can and staying as organized as possible. The phone calls are coming, and I'm ready for them! I am SO ready to bust out my "hip magician Santa" outfit again- complete with a beard, wig, and hat.
