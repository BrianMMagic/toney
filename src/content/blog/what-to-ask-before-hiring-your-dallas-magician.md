Planning a corporate event requires careful consideration when selecting entertainment. Whether you're an experienced event planner or new to the role, asking the right questions before hiring a magician matters significantly. The more questions, the better — here are six key ones to get you started.

## 1. What Size of Audience Are Your Magic Shows Right For?

A skilled magician should demonstrate versatility across different crowd sizes. They should be capable of commanding attention in large auditoriums for stage performances and offering intimate strolling magic for smaller gatherings. This flexibility allows them to meet diverse client needs effectively.

## 2. How Long Have You Been a Corporate Magician?

Experience matters, though newcomers shouldn't be automatically dismissed. Some magicians transition from other entertainment fields into magic. Understanding their background helps inform your hiring decision.

## 3. If I Hire You as a Corporate Magician, What Outcomes Can I Expect?

Quality entertainment should create moments of genuine surprise and connection. Audiences should experience laughter and amazement, with the performer helping break the ice between strangers. The goal includes transforming acquaintances into people "laughing together like they're old friends."

## 4. Are You Insured?

Professional magicians should carry insurance coverage. Some venues require vendors to carry liability protection. This protects both the client and the performer. Having insurance "shows that they care about their clients" in unexpected situations.

## 5. What Kind of Attire Do You Wear While Performing Corporate Magic?

Appearance requirements vary by event and company culture. Some events call for bold, eye-catching outfits that make performers stand out, while others prefer elegance or blending into the crowd. Discussing dress codes in advance prevents last-minute complications. A good magician adapts their wardrobe — from varied suit styles to specialized costumes — to match each event's specific requirements.

## 6. Do You Take Medical Precautions at Corporate Events?

Health considerations remain important for professional entertainers. Taking protective measures including vaccination, mask-wearing when requested, regular testing, and carrying sanitizer for guests assisting with tricks helps attendees feel secure and comfortable.
