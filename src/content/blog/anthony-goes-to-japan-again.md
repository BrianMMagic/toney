I'm so obsessed with Japan that I visited for the second time in 10 years. Here are some highlights from this incredible return trip.

## Osaka

My journey began in Osaka, where I rediscovered the city's vibrant atmosphere. The streets were always bustling with people and the food was just as delicious as I remembered. I sampled popular street fare including Takoyaki and Okonomiyaki, and toured Osaka Castle again, appreciating its historical significance and architectural beauty.

## Tokyo

In Tokyo, I was impressed by contemporary infrastructure and culinary experiences. I visited Shibuya Crossing and Tokyo Tower, noting that the train system facilitated easy navigation throughout the city. The standout element was the sushi, which I describe as "some of the best sushi I have ever had in my life."

## Kyoto

The trip concluded in Kyoto, known for preserving traditional elements. I visited Fushimi Inari Shrine and Kinkaku-ji Temple, both exceptionally beautiful, and participated in an authentic tea ceremony — a truly special experience.

My second journey proved equally rewarding as the first, offering fresh cultural and historical insights at every turn. Till next time! Sayonara!
