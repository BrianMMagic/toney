Here are some of the most frequently asked questions I receive from corporate and private event clients regarding my work as a magician and mentalist in the Dallas area.

## What attire do you wear for corporate events?

For formal occasions like awards banquets, I wear either a tailored suit or tuxedo. Tuxedo rentals can be arranged through local Dallas shops, and custom suiting options are available for those seeking personalized touches like monogramming.

## How many hours of strolling magic should I book?

The appropriate duration depends on multiple variables: "How many guests? Do these guests know one another?" along with venue type, competing entertainment, and scheduled speeches. Each event requires individual assessment rather than a standard formula.

## How do you engage quieter guests?

I politely include reserved attendees in group tricks, which naturally sparks conversation among participants. This approach ensures all guests feel welcomed without drawing unwanted attention.

## Will repeat clients see new tricks?

Yes, I maintain records of previous performances and "simply perform a different set" for returning clients. My routines continuously evolve with new material.

## Do you perform for children?

While I lack a dedicated children's routine, many "all-ages" tricks appeal equally to adults and young audiences, since magic's appeal transcends age and intelligence levels.

## Will you travel for multi-location events?

I travel nationally for corporate bookings when travel and accommodations are provided. Out-of-state performances frequently occur in New Orleans and Tennessee.

## What inspired your career choice?

Professional autonomy, travel opportunities, and the satisfaction of entertaining people motivated this career path. I appreciate the freedom to schedule personal travel without requesting vacation time.

## Is this your only profession?

Beyond magic, I work as an actor and voice actor. Recent work includes a Six Flags commercial demonstrating safety procedures, with aspirations toward cartoon voice work.

## What other events do you perform for?

My portfolio spans multiple categories:

**School Events:** graduation parties, after-prom gatherings, homecoming dances, assemblies, and fundraisers

**Business Events:** corporate meetings, sales conventions, employee appreciation, team building, leadership summits, and product launches

**Life Events:** milestone birthdays, retirements, bar/bat mitzvahs, weddings, holiday parties, and festival performances

Magic enhances all kinds of occasions — I'd love to help make your next event truly special!
