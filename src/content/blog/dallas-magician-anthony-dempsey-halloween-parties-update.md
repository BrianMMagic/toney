I hope everyone's enjoying these first few days of October- my favorite time of year! Not only is it the spookiest time of year, but it's also my birthday month, which I fully intend to celebrate all month long. That's what everyone does, right?

Anyway, I'm happy to announce that this year I'm offering a special magical appearance by none other than Beetlejuice! Complete with the black and white suit, wild hair, gross makeup, and killer jokes. I know I won't be able to come close to the performance Michael Keaton gave in the Beetlejuice film, but I'll try my best!

I have always been a fan of this character, and I was so bummed that Universal Studios Orlando retired the live Beetlejuice/ Universal Monsters show after so many successful years. But I'm sure whatever they replaced it with will do just as great. That's actually a joke because the ride that replaced it has been called by many, "The Worst Ride Ever". It's a shame! The show was really entertaining!

Speaking of entertainment, I've got two October parties booked as Dallas Magician Beetlejuice, and I'd love to make an appearance at yours to show your friends and family some hilarious magic and mentalism while they enjoy the party. I'll be strolling around the room from group to group, letting people decide whether they'd like to see some sleight of hand or have their minds read. Who knows, I may just have a few new creepy crawlies up my sleeve!

If you're having a Halloween party and you're in need of a Dallas magician, contact me today to see if I'm available on your party's date! I'd love to make an appearance in costume and show your guests a great time.
