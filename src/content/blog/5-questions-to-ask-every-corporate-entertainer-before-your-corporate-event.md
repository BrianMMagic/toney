When hiring entertainment for a corporate gathering, prospective clients should be equipped with thoughtful inquiries. Whether you're an employee organizing a business function or a seasoned event coordinator, asking the right questions ensures your 2025 event succeeds. This guide focuses on what to consider when booking a Dallas magician or similar corporate entertainer.

## 1. What size of audience do you perform for?

Professional corporate entertainers should demonstrate versatility across different group sizes. The ideal performer handles both intimate strolling magic for 20 people and elaborate stage productions for auditoriums seating 1,000+. Different corporate needs require different approaches to entertainment delivery.

## 2. How long have you been in corporate entertainment?

Experience matters, though newer performers shouldn't be dismissed outright. Some entertainers transition from wedding magic or college performances. Understanding your entertainer's background helps inform your hiring decision, even if they're establishing themselves in corporate work.

## 3. What outcomes can I expect from hiring you as my corporate event magician?

Anticipated results include engaged audiences who are "laughing, gasping, and occasionally letting out a scream." Quality entertainment breaks the ice, helps strangers connect, and creates memorable shared experiences among attendees.

## 4. Do you have insurance?

Professional entertainers should carry liability insurance. Some venues mandate vendor insurance before allowing performance. Securing this information prevents last-minute complications and demonstrates the performer's commitment to client protection.

## 5. What kind of attire do you wear for corporate events?

Different events require different presentation styles. Some companies prefer distinctive costumes for visibility, while others want entertainers blending in with business attire. Discussing wardrobe preferences ensures alignment with your event's vision and theme.

## 6. Do you take medical safety precautions at corporate events?

Reputable performers respect health considerations by offering masks upon request and carrying sanitizer for audience members involved in tricks. This demonstrates professionalism and consideration for attendee comfort.
