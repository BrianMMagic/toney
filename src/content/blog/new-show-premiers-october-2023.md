October proved to be an exceptionally hectic month. I juggled multiple corporate events, a milestone 75th birthday celebration, a teen entertainment night, and numerous wedding receptions. With a young daughter at home now, I barely had time to savor the seasonal treats — my wife and I used to make the annual pilgrimage to Halloween Horror Nights at Universal Studios Orlando, but we've postponed that tradition temporarily!

**Big News:** I've completed work on a brand new stage performance and am currently accepting bookings for private shows throughout 2025.

The production blends magic and mentalism with comedy and dramatic elements, emphasizing "chance and fate" while featuring substantial audience interaction designed to challenge perception and sensory awareness.

This flexible offering accommodates both traditional auditorium settings and intimate home environments like living rooms. I'm genuinely excited to observe audience reactions to this newest production — it's unlike anything I've done before.

If you're interested in booking a private show, reach out today to check availability and discuss details!
