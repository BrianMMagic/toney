2024 has started to get into a nice groove after a slower start, and private events have returned to normal levels. One recent highlight was a booking for strolling magic at a gender reveal celebration in Uptown Dallas at The Clutch Bar.

The venue was fantastic — it features an indoor dining area, expansive patio with turf flooring, a retractable roof, and an outdoor bar. The client wanted her event to stand out from typical celebrations, so she hired entertainment to help achieve that distinction. I moved between guest groups throughout the evening, performing magic and mentalism for everyone.

The event had a beautiful Alice in Wonderland aesthetic with balloon cascades, draped fabrics, and pastel linens at cocktail tables. Guests wore pink and blue, while the bar served champagne in matching colors. It was a truly stunning setup.

Beyond the magic performances, attendees enjoyed an open bar, specialty desserts, DJ music, and a popcorn machine. The group's energy was infectious — "Everyone was so energetic and full of laughter."

Congratulations to the expectant parents! If you're planning a gender reveal or private party in the Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex, I'd love to be part of your celebration.
