Throughout my career performing magic and mentalism across Dallas-Fort Worth, I've had the privilege of performing at some truly unforgettable events. Here are the seven most unique parties I've performed for — proof that creative themes make for the most memorable performances.

## 7. Hip Santa Office Christmas Party

A stylish office celebration featured me dressed as "hip Santa" in a red Lauren by Ralph Lauren suit, velvet Santa hat, and full beard. The event included a gift exchange and open bar — and I had a blast doing magic as hip Santa!

## 6. Edwardian-Era Wedding

Held in a historic mansion, this wedding featured me in period attire: burgundy tailcoat, top hat, and distinctive neckties. I performed a 30-minute after-dinner magic show that guests thoroughly enjoyed.

## 5. 70s Themed Party

Organized by Party On Events, this celebration captured authentic 1970s aesthetics with "peace signs, psychedelic colors, disco balls" and celebrity impersonators including Sonny and Cher lookalikes.

## 4. Witches' Tea Party

Planned by a repeat client at a private residence, this event featured Halloween decor and a liquid nitrogen ice cream vendor. I amplified my mind-reading performances to enhance the spooky atmosphere.

## 3. Mad Hatter Themed Party

Commissioned by Match.com, I debuted a custom-made violet velvet jacket (made in Portugal) while performing as The Mad Hatter. The venue featured an elaborate flower arrangement with dangling teacups — absolutely stunning.

## 2. New Year's Eve Wedding

A black-tie, minimalist wedding featuring gold accents and custom neon signage. The event culminated with a midnight countdown. I collaborated with fellow entertainer Brian Masters for this one.

## 1. Toy Themed Fundraiser

The most intensely themed event of my career, featuring guests dressed as favorite toys and me in classic magician attire (top hat, tuxedo, floor-length cape). The venue included a full-size Easy-Bake Oven, spiral slide into a ball pit, and giant Candy Land display. This fundraiser benefited The Birthday Party Project, which serves children in homeless and transitional housing — truly a special event for a wonderful cause.
